# Privacy Policy

## Pour Cast

**Effective date:** September 4, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 4, 2026

Thank you for using Pour Cast. Pour Cast is a weather-planning companion designed to help concrete professionals and homeowners plan pours around changing weather conditions. We believe useful tools should be respectful of the information entrusted to them. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information Pour Cast uses, why it uses it, where it is kept, and the choices available to you.

By using Pour Cast, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

> **At a glance:** Pour Cast has no account system, does not sell personal information, does not show third-party advertising, and does not use your data for cross-app tracking. Your sites, pour plans, settings, and downloaded forecasts are kept on your device. Location information is optional.

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

Pour Cast does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, password, payment information, contacts, photos, or messages.

We do not operate a user database for Pour Cast. We do not use the app to build advertising profiles, make marketing audiences, or track you across apps and websites.

## 2. Information Used by the App

The app uses only the information needed to provide the features you choose.

### Site information

You may create a site by searching for a place, selecting your current location, or entering a location manually. A site record can include a name, latitude, and longitude. This information lets the app request an accurate forecast for that site.

### Pour-planning information

When you plan or log a pour, Pour Cast may store information such as the site selected, planned start time, concrete element, mix type, protection choice, cure status, and weather-risk results. This information is used only to display your plans, calculate weather windows, and create the local alerts you enable.

### Forecast information

To assess conditions, Pour Cast downloads forecast information such as temperature, humidity, precipitation probability and amount, wind speed, and forecast time. Forecasts may be saved locally so the app can show results and evaluate cure risks without repeatedly requesting the same data.

### App preferences

The app stores preferences you choose, such as temperature units, workday hours, notification settings, selected weather provider, and the currently selected site. These preferences remain on your device.

## 3. Location Information

Location access is optional. If you tap **Use Current Location**, Pour Cast requests permission to access your location **while you are using the app**. The app uses a one-time location reading to create or name a site and to request the corresponding weather forecast.

You can use Pour Cast without granting location permission by searching for a city or adding a site manually. If permission is denied, the manual option remains available.

Pour Cast does not request background location access, does not continuously monitor your location, and does not create a travel or movement history.

You can change location permission at any time in **Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services** on your iPhone or iPad.

## 4. Notifications

With your permission, Pour Cast may schedule notifications on your device, including:

- a reminder before a planned pour;
- a notice that a suitable pour window may open the following day; and
- a frost or rain warning during an active cure period.

Notifications are optional. They are created and scheduled locally through Apple's notification system; Pour Cast does not send notification content to its own server. You can turn off notifications through the app or in your device's notification settings at any time.

## 5. When Information Is Shared Outside the App

Pour Cast shares limited information with third-party services only when it is necessary to deliver a feature you request. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

| Service | Information used or sent | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **OpenWeatherMap** | Latitude and longitude of the selected site | To retrieve a weather forecast when OpenWeatherMap is selected as the weather source. |
| **Apple Weather** | Latitude and longitude of the selected site | To retrieve a weather forecast when Apple Weather is selected as the weather source. |
| **Apple location and geocoding services** | A one-time device location, location coordinates, or a city/place search query | To provide current-location, reverse-geocoding, and city-search features. |
| **Apple notification services** | Notification content created on your device | To deliver alerts that you have allowed. |

When requesting a forecast, Pour Cast sends the site coordinates needed for the request. It does not intentionally include your site name, pour history, mix choices, protection choices, or app preferences in that request.

Network providers and third-party services may receive technical information that is normally transmitted when any device connects to an online service, such as IP address, device/network information, request time, and approximate region. Please review the applicable provider's privacy policy for details about its independent processing practices.

## 6. Local Storage and Data Retention

Pour Cast stores your app data locally in its application storage on your device. This can include sites, plans and logged pours, forecasts, calculated risks, and preferences.

Your information remains there until you remove it. You can delete a site in the app; its locally stored forecast data is deleted with it. You can also remove the app to remove its locally stored app data. Device backups, shared files, or operating-system retention may be governed by Apple and your own device settings.

## 7. Exporting and Sharing Your Data

Pour Cast includes an option to export your app data as a JSON file. An export can contain your sites, pour records, saved forecasts, and preferences. The export is created only after you choose the export action.

You control whether, where, and with whom the exported file is shared. Once you share it through email, cloud storage, messaging, or another app, that recipient's privacy practices apply. Please review the file before sharing it, as site coordinates and pour details may be sensitive in your context.

## 8. Data Sales, Advertising, and Tracking

Pour Cast does not sell personal information. It does not use third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral advertising, or cross-app and cross-website tracking. We do not use your information to serve targeted ads.

## 9. Security

We rely on the security features provided by iOS and your device to protect locally stored app data. Weather and location-service requests are made over encrypted network connections when supported by the service.

No method of electronic storage or data transmission is completely secure. While we work to use reasonable safeguards, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information stored on a device or sent over the internet.

## 10. Children’s Privacy

Pour Cast is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact the developer through the support channel listed on the Pour Cast App Store page.

## 11. Your Choices and Controls

You remain in control of the information used by Pour Cast. You can:

- use manual site search instead of current location;
- allow, deny, or revoke location permission in your device settings;
- allow, disable, or customize notifications in your device settings;
- delete sites and locally stored forecast data in the app;
- delete pour records in the app; and
- export your data only when you choose to do so.

Because Pour Cast does not maintain user accounts or a central user-data database, we generally cannot access, correct, or delete data stored only on your device for you.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features, legal requirements, or data practices change. When we do, we will post the revised version and change the **Last updated** date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

## 13. Contact Us

If you have a privacy question, concern, or request about Pour Cast, please contact the developer through the support channel listed on the Pour Cast App Store product page.
